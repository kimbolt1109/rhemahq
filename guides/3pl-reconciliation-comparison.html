How to choose

  • You live in Shopify + ShipBob and want automated checks: Cartwright is built for exactly that, at the same $49 price. It connects accounts and runs eight discrepancy checks. If that is your whole stack, it is a strong fit.
  • You want a findings-based audit across providers and will pay for it: WareAudit's $149/mo or $299 one-off covers any-3PL CSV with dispute-oriented reports.
  • You are already paying for SKU.io sync: its bill grouping is included; you do not need another tool for rollups.
  • You have multiple 3PLs, are not on Shopify, cannot connect APIs, or need the original row address for credit disputes: the offline workbook is the only option here that needs no account connection and keeps file/worksheet/line + hash per row.

What our tool does not do

It does not detect overcharges automatically, connect to Shopify or ShipBob, or decide that a charge is wrong. It consolidates and preserves evidence so a human can dispute fast. If you want automated discrepancy detection, buy Cartwright or WareAudit — that is their job, and they do it.

About those "3–15% leakage" numbers

Every error-rate statistic in this market is vendor-reported. WareAudit's own site states no independent study exists. We therefore sell hours saved at close and faster credit disputes, not a recovery percentage. Measure your own baseline before believing anyone's number, including ours.

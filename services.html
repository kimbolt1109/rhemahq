Services

Done-for-you, same receipts.

If you would rather hand us the files than run the tool yourself, we offer three fixed-scope engagements. Each returns the finished artifact plus the source receipt — original file, worksheet or line, and SHA-256 per row — exactly as the tools produce.

Fulfillment close pack — USD 120

Up to 12 invoice files / 6,000 billed rows from any 3PL (CSV or XLSX). Returns one reconciliation workbook: fee rollup by provider, month, currency and label, plus a source-reference sheet. Turnaround: 2 business days after we receive the files.

Journal galley pack — USD 200 per article

One accepted Word manuscript plus metadata. Returns two-column PDF, standalone HTML and DTD-valid JATS Publishing 1.2 XML, registered as galleys on your OJS 3.5 submission (or delivered as files for your production editor). Turnaround: 3 business days. Complex layouts are flagged for editor review, not silently approximated.

Moodle bank release — USD 250

Up to 10 exported Moodle XML banks / 500 questions. Returns one merged import file, counts per category, and a per-question receipt (category, id, source line, checksum). Duplicate ids are rejected and reported. Turnaround: 2 business days.

How it works

  1. Email support@rhemahq.com with the scope above and your file counts.
  2. We confirm scope and a fixed price in writing before any work. You authorize us to process the files you send.
  3. Payment via Polar (merchant of record); invoice with your institution/company details available in the customer portal.
  4. Each engagement includes a license key for the corresponding tool, valid for the engagement plus 30 days, so your team can re-run or extend the work in-house.

Limits we keep

We do not make accounting postings, tax filings, grading decisions or editorial judgments. We serialize and tie out what you supplied; your qualified staff approve the result. Files are handled only for the engagement and deleted on request.

Service capacity is limited (a handful of engagements per month). If the queue is full we will say so and offer the self-serve tool instead.

Policies

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Polar as merchant of record. Polar handles payment, tax and the refund transaction; we handle the product and your license.

How to request a refund

  1. Reply to your Polar receipt email, or use the customer portal link in that email, and request a refund from Polar.
  2. You can also write to support@trimprocure.com and we will route the request to Polar with your order reference.

Polar's buyer terms govern the transaction. Polar may grant discretionary refunds up to 60 days after purchase; statutory consumer rights in your country are unaffected.

What happens to your license

  • On a refunded order, the associated license key is revoked and commercial use of the tool must stop.
  • For subscriptions, cancellation stops future billing at the end of the paid period unless Polar refunds the current period.
  • Files you already produced with the tool remain yours. The tools run locally; we never hold your invoices, manuscripts or question banks, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Before you buy

Every tool has a free evaluation download and a live browser demo at rhemahq.com. Run your own representative file through the evaluation first — it is the same code path as the licensed tool. If it does not fit your workflow, you have lost nothing.

Refund processing fees charged by the payment network are not returned to us by Polar; this does not change the amount Polar refunds you under its terms.

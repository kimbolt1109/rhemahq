Policies

Refunds

For browser-tool licenses purchased through Polar, Polar handles payments, tax and refunds as merchant of record. We handle the product and your license.

How to request a refund

  1. Reply to your Polar receipt email, or use the customer portal link in that email, and request a refund from Polar.
  2. You can also write to support@rhemahq.com and we will route the request to Polar with your order reference.

Polar's buyer terms govern the transaction. Polar may grant discretionary refunds up to 60 days after purchase; statutory consumer rights in your country are unaffected.

What happens to your license

  • On a refunded order, the associated license key is revoked and commercial use of the tool must stop.
  • For subscriptions, cancellation stops future billing at the end of the paid period unless Polar refunds the current period.
  • Files you already produced with a self-serve tool remain yours. The software processes those files on your device.

Before you buy

Try Fulfillment Ledger and Assessment Release Pack with a sample or your own files in the browser demos. Galley Studio is available under the license in its download.

Refund processing fees charged by the payment network are not returned to us by Polar; this does not change the amount Polar refunds you under its terms.

Policies

Privacy

The browser tools process files on your device. This page covers how the site, Polar checkout and service engagements handle data.

The tools

  • File processing is local. Invoices, manuscripts and question banks are read in your browser (or by the local scripts) and are not uploaded to us or to Polar.
  • No accounts are required on this site, and we set no advertising or tracking cookies.

This website

  • Static pages served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs standard request metadata (IP, user agent) under its own privacy statement.
  • Fonts and the spreadsheet library are served with this site. The homepage can load its optional 3D renderer from jsDelivr, which receives standard request metadata.
  • The homepage keeps interaction events in memory. It sends them to an analytics service only when one is configured. The site currently ships without an analytics provider or advertising scripts.

Checkout

Payment is handled entirely by Polar as merchant of record. Polar collects the billing and payment data needed to process the order, calculate tax and issue your receipt and invoice, under Polar's privacy policy. We receive order metadata (product, amount, status) and the contact email you provide, which we use for support and license delivery.

Support email

If you write to support@trimprocure.com, we keep the correspondence to answer you and to improve the product. We do not sell or share it. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Files you send us

If you send files for a service engagement, we use them for that engagement. You can request deletion by emailing support.

Retention and rights

Order records are retained as required for tax and accounting through Polar. You may request access, correction or deletion of correspondence we hold by emailing support.

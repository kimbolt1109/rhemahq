Policies

Privacy

The short version: your files never leave your device. The tools read and write locally in your browser or on your machine. This page lists everything that does touch a network.

The tools

  • File processing is local. Invoices, manuscripts and question banks are read in your browser (or by the local scripts) and are not uploaded to us or to Polar.
  • License validation (licensed commercial use only) sends the organization identifier, the benefit identifier and your license key to Polar's validation endpoint. It does not send file contents, filenames or row data.
  • No accounts are required on this site, and we set no advertising or tracking cookies.

This website

  • Static pages served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs standard request metadata (IP, user agent) under its own privacy statement.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts and the demo spreadsheet library from jsDelivr; those providers receive standard request metadata.
  • Event instrumentation on this site queues interactions locally and forwards them only to a first-party analytics endpoint if one is configured. No third-party analytics or advertising scripts are loaded by default.

Checkout

Payment is handled entirely by Polar as merchant of record. Polar collects the billing and payment data needed to process the order, calculate tax and issue your receipt and invoice, under Polar's privacy policy. We receive order metadata (product, amount, status) and the contact email you provide, which we use for support and license delivery.

Support email

If you write to support@trimprocure.com, we keep the correspondence to answer you and to improve the product. We do not sell or share it. Ask us to delete it at any time.

Retention and rights

Order records are retained as required for tax and accounting through Polar. You may request access, correction or deletion of correspondence we hold by emailing support. Because file processing is local, there is no file content held by us to access or delete.
